# Code of Conduct

TraceGate is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background, or affiliation.

Participants must use respectful language, assume good faith, give actionable feedback, and protect private or sensitive information. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, doxxing, and publishing credentials or private application data.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community. Report concerns privately through the repository's security or maintainer contact channel.
